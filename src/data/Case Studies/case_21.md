**. WHY IS AGE IMPORTANT?**

Age is an important cerebrovascular risk factor.

At **72 years**, the patient has an increased likelihood of:

- Atherosclerotic disease

- Carotid artery stenosis

- Hypertension

- Diabetes mellitus

- Reduced cerebrovascular reserve

- Small-vessel disease

In patients with significant carotid disease, cerebral blood flow may already be close to the lower limit required for normal neuronal function.

### Clinical Correlation

Watershed regions are located at the **distal junctions between major cerebral arterial territories**.

They are particularly vulnerable when cerebral perfusion falls because they receive relatively limited blood flow.

A sudden fall in systemic blood pressure can therefore produce ischemia in these **border-zone areas**.
