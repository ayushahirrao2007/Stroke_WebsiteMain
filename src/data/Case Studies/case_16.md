## 8. WHY IS AGE IMPORTANT?

Although lacunar strokes may occur at any age, they become more common after **50 years** because of progressive **small vessel disease** caused by chronic vascular risk factors.

Long-standing hypertension leads to:

- Lipohyalinosis

- Arteriolosclerosis

- Narrowing of small penetrating arteries

- Reduced blood supply to deep brain structures

The **basis pontis** receives blood from **small perforating branches of the basilar artery**, making it particularly susceptible to lacunar infarction.

### Clinical Correlation

A tiny infarct in the **left basis pontis** interrupts:

- Corticobulbar fibres → **Slurred speech (dysarthria)**

- Corticospinal fibres controlling the hand → **Clumsy hand and impaired fine motor function**
