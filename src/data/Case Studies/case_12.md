## 4. DOCTOR'S THOUGHT PROCESS

## 7. WHY IS AGE IMPORTANT?

The risk of ischemic stroke increases significantly after **55 years of age** due to progressive atherosclerosis and vascular degeneration.

Older adults have an increased risk of:

- Vertebrobasilar atherosclerosis

- Posterior circulation ischemic stroke

- Cardioembolic events

- Poor neurological recovery if diagnosis is delayed

Because cerebellar strokes may initially appear less dramatic than cortical strokes, delayed diagnosis in elderly patients can lead to life-threatening complications such as **brainstem compression and obstructive hydrocephalus**.

## 37. SECONDARY PREVENTION

The patient and family are counselled regarding:

- Strict blood pressure control

- Lipid management

- Smoking cessation

- Healthy balanced diet

- Weight management

- Regular physical activity after medical clearance

- Medication adherence

- Regular neurology follow-up

- Recognition of recurrent stroke symptoms using **BE-FAST**

- Immediate hospital presentation if new neurological symptoms develop

## 38. DISCHARGE SUMMARY

## Final Diagnosis

**Acute Right Posterior Inferior Cerebellar Artery (PICA) Territory Ischemic Stroke secondary to atherosclerotic vertebrobasilar disease.**
