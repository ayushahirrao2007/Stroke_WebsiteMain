- Conscious and fully oriented

- Neurologically intact

- Speech normal

- No facial weakness

- Normal motor power in all limbs

- Hemodynamically stable

- **NIHSS = 0**

**Discharge Advice**

- Take prescribed medications exactly as advised.

- Never stop anticoagulant therapy without consulting your physician.

- Monitor blood pressure and blood glucose regularly.

- Follow a heart-healthy, low-salt diet.

- Maintain regular physical activity after medical clearance.

- Keep scheduled follow-up appointments with Neurology and Cardiology.

- Seek immediate emergency care if any of the following occur:

  - Sudden weakness or numbness

  - Slurred speech or difficulty speaking

  - Facial drooping

  - Sudden vision loss

  - Loss of balance or coordination
