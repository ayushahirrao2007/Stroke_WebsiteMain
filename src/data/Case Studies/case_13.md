## 7. WHY IS AGE IMPORTANT?

Although lacunar stroke may occur in middle-aged adults, its incidence increases after **55 years** because of progressive **small-vessel disease**.

Chronic hypertension and diabetes gradually damage the small penetrating arteries supplying the:

- Internal capsule

- Basal ganglia

- Thalamus

- Pons

This process, known as **lipohyalinosis**, makes these vessels susceptible to occlusion, resulting in **lacunar infarction**.
