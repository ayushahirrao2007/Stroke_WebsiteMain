## 7. WHY IS AGE IMPORTANT?

The incidence of **Transient Ischaemic Attack (TIA)** increases significantly after **60 years** because of progressive atherosclerosis and age-related vascular changes.

Older adults have a higher risk of:

- Carotid artery stenosis

- Cerebral atherosclerosis

- Cardioembolic events

- Small vessel disease

- Progression from TIA to completed ischemic stroke

Studies have shown that the risk of a completed stroke is highest during the **first 48 hours** after a TIA, making early diagnosis and treatment essential.
